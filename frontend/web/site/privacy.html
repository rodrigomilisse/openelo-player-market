Privacy

Draft. A plain-language starting point, to be reviewed before launch.

What we collect

  • Waitlist: your email address and country, only if you join the waitlist. We use them to tell you when the market opens where you live.
  • Visits: anonymous page counts (which pages, from which country, on what kind of device), with no cookies and nothing that identifies you.
  • In your browser only: your light or dark choice and the play-money market account are stored on your device and never sent to us.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your email, we don't run advertising trackers, and we don't build profiles.

Your choices

Every waitlist email has an unsubscribe link. To have your email deleted, write to us from the About page. Clearing your browser's site data removes everything stored on your device.
