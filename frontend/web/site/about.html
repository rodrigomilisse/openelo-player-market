About OpenElo

Open player ratings from match results and minutes played. One number per player that anyone can check.

Why

Player ratings today are opinions: scouts, votes, fantasy points, transfer fees. They can't be checked and they disagree. OpenElo takes the idea that already works for teams and chess players, Elo, and applies it to footballers using only what is public and objective: who won, who played, for how long.

What makes it different

  • Only results and minutes. No scouts, no votes, no transfer fees, no betting odds.
  • Open. The formula and its code are public. Anyone can recompute every number. How it works
  • Every player, every league. Ratings travel between leagues through the matches clubs play against each other.

What's next

A market where fans hold shares in players and earn as their ratings hold up. It isn't open yet; the preview runs on play money. Join the waitlist to hear when it opens in your country.
