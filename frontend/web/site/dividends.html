Dividends

One pool pays the whole market. Each day a small share of it goes out, split by every held share's rating weight.

Mock model. Pool size, drain and payouts are illustrative. The real rules are still being set.

How your slice is worked out

his weight = 10rating ÷ 400  ·  your payout = today's payout × your weight ÷ all weight held

A player rated 400 points higher pays ten times as much per share. What you paid for the share doesn't matter, only his rating, and how much weight everyone else holds. When more fans buy, each share's slice gets a little thinner; when ratings across the market fall, a share in a player who held his rating is worth relatively more.
